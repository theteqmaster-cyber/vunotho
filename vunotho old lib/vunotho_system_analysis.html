🌱 1. System Overview & Problem Statement

Vunotho is not a passive produce listing board or basic farm management utility. It is an integrated Farmer-to-Value Operating System designed to solve the structural bottlenecks faced by rural smallholder farmers in emerging economies (starting with Zimbabwe !1).

👥 2. Stakeholder & Role-Based Actor Matrix

The system provides tailored interfaces and workflows based on distinct actor roles:

Actor Role Primary Responsibilities Core Capabilities Connectivity Profile
Farmer Registers harvest, reviews transparent net returns, requests pickup. Create harvest listings, compare buyer offers, schedule aggregated transport, view digital transaction ledger. Offline-First (Intermittent 2G/3G connectivity).
Buyer (Commercial / Retail) Posts verified demand requests, approves batches, issues payments. Submit crop specs, order aggregated supply, track delivery milestones, review supplier quality metrics. Continuous Internet (Web / Mobile).
Transporter / Driver Executes route pickups, verifies load weight/grade at farmgate, delivers to hubs. Receive dispatched route assignments, scan/verify manifests, update live GPS/status checkpoints !2. Semi-Connected (Mobile app with offline cache).
Vunotho Hub Administrator Oversees marketplace balance, routes surplus to processing, tracks Enactus KPIs. Dispatch routes, manage value-recovery facilities, resolve disputes, export verified impact analytics. Continuous Internet (High-density Admin Portal).

❓ 8. Missing Information & Clarification Questions

To transition from architectural analysis into implementation without assumptions, the following numbered items ([!1] - [!9]) require explicit clarification:

!1 Target Geographic Location & Currency Handling
The blueprint mentions Zimbabwe and international competitions (Enactus). Which target currency or dual-currency model should the platform calculate and display (e.g., USD $, ZiG, or multi-currency)?
!2 Transporter Matching & GPS Capability
In rural collection areas where cell towers or GPS accuracy may fluctuate, how should collection points be designated? Via precise GPS coordinates, predefined community collection hubs, or manual landmark drop-points?
!3 Offline Price Offer Expiry & Conflict Rules
If a farmer accepts a cached buyer offer while offline, but the buyer fills their quota or retracts the offer before the farmer re-establishes internet, what is the automated fallback policy for "Needs Attention"? Should the system auto-match the next best offer or prompt the farmer?
!4 Payment Settlement Mechanism
How are payments transferred to farmers upon completion? Via mobile money (e.g., EcoCash, InnBucks), cash on farmgate collection by transporter, bank transfer, or internal Vunotho digital escrow balances?
!5 Transport Fee Splitting Logic
When the logistics engine aggregates loads from 3 different farmers into one truck: is the transport fee divided proportionally by weight (kg), distance from hub, or flat rate per pickup?
!6 Processing Pathway Operation Model
Are the Vunotho Processing Centres physical facilities owned and operated directly by the Enactus team/cooperative, or are they 3rd-party processing partners connected via the platform?
!7 Role Permissions & User Sign-up Flow
Can a farmer register themselves directly via the app, or is registration carried out in person by community field agents/Enactus team members to guarantee verified profiles?
!8 Communication Channels for Non-Smartphone Farmers
Is the platform strictly a smartphone application (PWA/Android), or should it support auxiliary SMS / USSD / WhatsApp bot bridges for farmers without smartphones in the pilot community?
!9 Enactus Competition Presentation & Interactive Demo Mode
Do you require a built-in "Enactus Judge / Demo Walkthrough Mode" with preloaded pilot data (showing stage 1 potato farmer income before vs. after, kg saved from waste, and live map aggregation) to demonstrate directly during competition pitches?
